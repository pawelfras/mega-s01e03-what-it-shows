The three problems

parser — the shared parser read every date month first. Day-first tenants got day and month swapped: “7 April” went out as “4 July”. This is what #4471’s title describes. Fixed.

hint — booking-v3 printed MM/DD/YYYY under its date field. At day-first tenants on booking-v3, people typed what the hint said, month first, and the form read it day first. It looked like the parser bug in the reports, but the parser fix does not touch it. Won’t fix: the form is retired.

one-day — the date moves by one day, not a month flip. Tuesday shows as Monday, an invite arrives before the booking, two clients in different time zones get different days for the same meeting. That is a time-zone or day-boundary bug. The desk noted these reports (desk-notes.json) and counts them where they are filed, under parser. The parser fix does not reach them. Still open.

Do not count by intake tag. Intake guesses the cause from the reporter's browser; many reports come from browsers set to en-US at day-first tenants. The cause follows the tenant's locale, its booking form and the channel (causes.ts), and the "intake tag differs" column shows how often the guess is wrong.

How this was counted

  1. Drop repeated rows: the export pages overlap, so one id counts once (uniqueById).
  2. Place every report with causeOf (groupByCause): day-first tenant, form channel, booking-v3 → hint; any other day-first report → parser.
  3. List the desk’s one-day reports again as their own problem; they stay counted under parser, as the desk files them.

What to change on the desk

  • Split by cause, not subject. Same subject, different cause: keep apart. The dedupe explorer already shows the difference.
  • When a big ticket is closed, check that its fixes cover every problem in it.
