The three problems

parser — the shared parser reads every date month first. Day-first tenants get day and month swapped: “7 April” goes out as “4 July”. This is what #4471’s title describes, and its queued fix covers only this.

hint — booking-v3 prints MM/DD/YYYY under its date field. At day-first tenants on booking-v3, people type what the hint says, month first, and the form reads it day first. It looks like the parser bug in the report, but the parser fix will not touch it: the form’s hint and its reading must agree.

one-day — the date moves by one day, not a month flip. Tuesday shows as Monday, an invite arrives before the booking, two clients in different time zones get different days for the same meeting. That is a time-zone or day-boundary bug. The desk noted these reports (desk-notes.json) but the #4471 table counts them under parser.

Do not count by intake tag. Intake guesses the cause from the reporter's browser; many reports come from browsers set to en-US at day-first tenants. The cause follows the tenant's locale, its booking form and the channel (causes.ts), and the "intake tag differs" column shows how often the guess is wrong.

How this was counted

  1. Drop repeated rows: the export pages overlap, so one id counts once (uniqueById).
  2. Take the desk’s one-day list first; those reports are one-day.
  3. Place every other report with causeOf: day-first tenant, form channel, booking-v3 → hint; any other day-first report → parser.

What to change on the desk

  • Split by cause, not subject. Same subject, different cause: keep apart. The dedupe explorer already shows the difference.
  • When a big ticket is marked known, check that its fix covers every report in it.
